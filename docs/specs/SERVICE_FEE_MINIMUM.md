# Service fee minimum (from Aaron, 2026-11-08; planned, not started)

Every estimate and job gets a last line, **Service fee minimum**, that tops the labor up to a minimum.

- The minimum equals one hour of labor, but it is never labeled or described that way. It is just "Service fee minimum".
- It is dynamic: the line is the minimum minus the labor on the other lines, never below zero.
  Example: labor lines total $40 and the minimum is $100, so the line becomes $60. If labor is $100 or more, the line is $0 and is hidden or shown as $0 (to decide).
- Every estimate states, in plain words, that a labor service fee minimum applies, and what it is (for example "A labor service fee minimum of $100 applies to this estimate").
- Some customers are exempt. A setting on the customer ("No service fee minimum") turns it off for that customer; their estimates then show no line and no statement.
- The minimum amount is a company setting (the current rate is $120 per hour; Aaron to confirm the starting amount).
